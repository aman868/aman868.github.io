Privacy Policy for Neon Blast

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Gridline Studios ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Neon Blast mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you play our game and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

While playing Neon Blast, we do not directly collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

However, our application incorporates third-party services (such as Google AdMob) which automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical information to serve advertisements and optimize game performance.

Types of Data Collected by Third-Party Providers:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID for Android, IDFA for iOS), IP address, device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
  • Usage & Diagnostic Data: App launch frequency, session duration, gameplay crash logs, and ad interaction data.

2. Third-Party Advertising Services

Our game uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, app open, and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect and use information to personalize ads according to Google's Advertising Policies.

You can learn more about how Google processes data by visiting:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information From Apps That Use Their Services

3. Children's Privacy

Neon Blast does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can remove such information.

4. Data Security & Storage

We value your trust. All third-party SDK partners retain diagnostic and advertising data in strict compliance with international data privacy regulations including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA. Gridline Studios does not store user personal data on external private servers.

5. Your Rights & Ad Personalization Opt-Out

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time via your mobile device settings:

  • Android: Go to Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads ➔ Reset Advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Go to Settings ➔ Privacy & Security ➔ Tracking ➔ Toggle off Allow Apps to Request to Track.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.
